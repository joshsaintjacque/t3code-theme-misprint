Your agent runs the press.
The inks slip while it works.

A two-ink theme for T3 Code: fluoro pink and teal on cream stock. While a turn runs the plates drift out of register and clack between offsets. When it stops they snap back into place, and the overlap goes deep indigo.

Run the press

This is the actual mechanism, on the actual art. Start a turn and the two plates slip; finish it and they snap home. The wordmark and the dashed rules on this page follow along.

At rest · registered

Idle is still: both inks sit exactly on top of each other. Working is a press that never quite locks. On completion the transition overshoots, like a registration pin seating.

What moves

Every animation is tied to app state. If nothing is running, nothing moves.

01 · WORKING

Gutter plates

On wide windows the margins hold a halftone test print. Each ink is its own layer; during a turn they clack out of register, then settle.

02 · WORKING

Header rule

The dashed rule under the header is printed in both inks. They drift apart while the agent runs and overprint dark when it stops.

03 · BACKGROUND

Busy threads

Any thread with an agent on it gets two dashed rules marching in opposite directions. Same under the live “Working…” row.

04 · FOCUS

Composer

At rest the box has a pink offset plate. Focus it and the teal plate prints on the other corner.

05 · HOVER

Thread rows

Pink grows from the left, teal from the right, and the overlap goes dark where they meet.

06 · NARROW

Docked sheet

No gutter at your window size? A small proof sheet docks above the sidebar footer instead, with the same slip.

Reduced motion freezes everything to the registered still. Only transforms, opacity, background-position and registered custom properties are animated.
